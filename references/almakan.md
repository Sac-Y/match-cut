# The reference film: AlMakan Group 10 Year Anniversary Video

The target for every montage this skill makes. Match its execution (relays, motion, rhythm, discipline). Its story
angle and its templates are one good example, not a rule (see planning.md). The film is 81 s, 138 shots, measured
frame by frame; it is not shipped with the skill, so everything needed is written down here. The two films in
`examples/` show the style applied to products.

## What it is

"Ten years, many restaurants, one group." The same action or the same shape is relayed between different
restaurants, kitchens and people. At the cut the world changes but the motion does not stop, so each cut rhymes
instead of being a transition effect.

## Story structure

| Chapter | Time | Pictures | What the relay chain says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin | 0–9 s | wristwatch, whisky glass over blueprints, wall clock, prep and delivery | time: ten years start here |
| The places | 9–14 s | dining room, kitchen, a storefront at night, laying blocks | restaurants being built |
| Craft and service | 13–30 s | guests eating ↔ kitchen; stamp → coaster (each restaurant's mark); plate set to centre (a new tablecloth = a new restaurant); knife / pan / mallet swings | many brands, one craft |
| Growth | 30–41 s | writing recipes, owner checks his watch, team briefing, passport stamp, map, new concepts, a press article | expansion, being noticed |
| Breath | 41.8–45 s | burner ring → iris; "AND / LET / THE / MAGIC / HAPPEN" word by word in the pupil; music drops | the theme line |
| Climax | 45–70 s | airborne chain, overhead roll through signature dishes, 8-dish lineup, cloche → egg dome, drop / press, upside-down flips, final burst | the portfolio, energy |
| Payoff | 70–81 s | 3.4 s black and silence → staff and guests at one long table, "TEN YEARS SERVING YOU, THANKS TO YOU" → logo on black | the people from before return |

## Three camera templates, reused (AlMakan's; a film may define its own)

| Template | Look | Where |
|---|---|---|
| A overhead centre | straight down, round subject at frame centre; the camera itself rolls slowly and the next shot keeps rolling the same way | 0–3.5 s, 6–8 s, 20–28 s, 47.7–51.3 s, 68–70 s |
| B tabletop side | camera near the counter, its edge across the lower middle, hands enter from left or right | 3.5–5 s, 16–17.9 s, 29–33 s, 60 s, 68.6 s |
| C canted lineup | ~12° dutch, low; the table makes a trapezoid in the lower half; the dish sits on the same spot; a hand comes in from above; one beat per dish and restaurant, camera fixed | 54.57–57.76 s (8 dishes, ~0.4 s each) |

Once the viewer has learned a template, every new dish or restaurant on it reads instantly. The templates are what
make the film feel controlled.

Rule: **cut while the action is half done, never after it ends.** The incoming shot is already moving.

## Motion (why the relays feel like one movement)

- **Every motion has a visible cause.** A hand turns, presses, places, throws; gravity drops; nothing moves by
  itself. The grip is the grip a person really uses for that object.
- **Direction and speed carry across the cut.** The knife's arc continues as the pan's arc; the push that rolls the
  bread rolls the dough the same way at the same pace. A relay never reverses direction mid-motion.
- **Real time.** Gestures play at natural speed; the energy comes from the cut rate, not from speeding clips up.
- **Every shot moves.** No shot holds still after its action; the cut comes before the motion settles.
- **Each object reads at once.** A watch is plainly a watch, a stamp plainly a stamp, in under half a second.

## Rhythm (measured)

- About 152 BPM (beat ≈ 0.395 s). Median shot 0.79 s (2 beats). Lineup: 1 beat per cut. Breaths (eye 41.8–44.9 s,
  egg 57.8–59.4 s): about 4 beats.
- Loudness: light and building to 35 s, clearly louder after 36 s, drops under the eye text (42–44 s), 3.4 s of
  black near-silence before the finale. The black works here because a new chapter (the people) follows in an
  81 s film; in a spot under 30 s it reads as the film breaking (see planning.md).

## Look

- Deep black base, one hard pool of top light. Kitchens lean teal, tables warm orange. This is AlMakan's own look,
  right for a restaurant at night; it is not a house style. Copy its discipline (one light direction held across
  each chain, a controlled palette, one film texture over everything); design each film's environments and light
  from its own concept (planning.md §5).
- Hands are the protagonists; faces are rare until the finale.
- The camera often cants or turns upside down (64.9–66.5 s): the rotation is part of the energy.

## Text

Words appear only twice: the theme line in the breath, and the thanks in the payoff. Both word by word, white serif
caps, centred.

## Why its cuts hold (what to carry into any angle)

1. **One angle, one world logic.** Every shot is a real moment in this group's life: founders, builders, chefs,
   waiters, guests, press. Nothing is borrowed from an unrelated world just for its shape.
2. **The matched thing belongs in both shots.** The watch is worn to tell the time; the glass is in hand while
   reading plans; the stamp prints the restaurant's own mark. No prop is placed only to rhyme.
3. **Each chain says one thing.** Circles say time, stamps say brands, plates say service, airborne says energy.
   The shape is the means; the meaning is the point.
4. **What is introduced pays off.** The briefing staff and the crowd from above sit at the final table.
5. **Few words, at the two moments that need them.**
