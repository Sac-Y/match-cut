# Example: Big Mac, "Noon Relay" (22.4 s)

`preview.mp4` is a 720p copy of the delivered film (v6); `montage.json` is its edit config in the skill's format
(its `dl/` sources are the project's downloads and are not shipped). An unofficial spec film made while this skill
was being written; not affiliated with or endorsed by McDonald's. The VO uses McDonald's own ingredient line, so a
real release would need the rights to it.

## The film

- **Angle:** many people, one moment. At noon, everyday objects across a city hand their shape or motion to the
  Big Mac being built in a kitchen, and the people from those worlds end up holding the burger.
- **Format:** 22.4 s, 1920×1080, 25 fps, 150 BPM, 560 frames.
- **Voice-over:** the ingredient list ("Two all-beef patties, special sauce …") is the metronome: each ingredient
  lands on its shot.

| Frames | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–60 | Opener | circles, top-down with a camera roll: a wristwatch → a vinyl record → a kitchen wall clock |
| 60–320 | Relays | world gesture → kitchen ingredient, one per VO word: closing a laptop → the grill press; a signature → the sauce; confetti → lettuce; a sticky note → cheese; a coin → a pickle; a hard hat set down → the top bun |
| 320–360 | Macro pull-out | the finished burger pulls back into a pool of light at exactly its lineup size and place |
| 360–453 | Lineup | eight noon worlds, one beat each: EVERY NOON. (no hands) then EVERY HAND. (a different hand touches it each beat); the last hand lifts it and the camera whips up |
| 453–520 | Hero | lands on the hero shot; ONE BIG MAC. |
| 520–560 | End card | logo on the song's own final hit |

## What it shows well

- A circle opener on round subjects with one continuous camera roll.
- A VO list used as the edit's metronome.
- The macro pull-out into the lineup framing, the lineup on one fixed spot, and a whip instead of a black slug.
- A song cut on its bar grid with its own ending kept whole, and voice-band ducking so the band keeps its weight.

## What not to copy

- **Props placed only to rhyme** (a coin for a pickle slice, confetti for lettuce): the shapes match but the cut
  reads as forced.
- **A builder introduced with a hard hat who never pays off:** the site returns in the lineup, but no builder's
  hand ever reaches the burger.
- **Exposure steps over 10% at 13 cuts** (measured in v5) between bright offices and dark kitchen shots: the dark
  kitchen look was AlMakan's, copied instead of designed for a noon story.
- **Fixed in later versions:** a second patty appearing from nowhere, and a black slug before the hero that read as
  the film breaking.

These became the first rules of the skill (planning.md §9).
